# Photographs for "Chaku Recipe: How to Make Maghe Sankranti Chaku at Home"

**Step photography is not optional on this page.** It carries Recipe schema,
and the image carousel at the top of a recipe search result is where nearly
all the clicks go. A recipe page without step photos competes with one hand
tied behind its back.

Alt text should describe the step and its visual cue, not just the object.
"Jaggery syrup forming a soft thread in cold water, the thread stage for
chaku" beats "chaku being made".

## Timing

This page needs to be live and photographed well before mid-January. Search
engines need weeks to rank a seasonal page ahead of its peak, and a recipe
page with no step images will not win the carousel no matter how early it goes
up.
